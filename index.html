Zakk Ziegler

Porco Dio Art

March 10, 1992 – August 19, 2026

Zakk was driven by an appreciation of art in all its forms – painting, music, film, and the written word. Self-taught and prolific, he shared his love of painting by teaching color theory to schoolchildren through the Greene County Parks and Recreation, and he made his own music from guitar loops, synthesizer samples, and found audio. Above all, he was a devoted father to his son, Lucien.
